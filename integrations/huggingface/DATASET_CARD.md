# ModelBoundary Lab Evidence Dataset

## Summary
Sanitized, versioned evidence records and protocol metadata for the ModelBoundary Lab research framework.

## DOI
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23442798

## Dataset status
Before publishing, replace this template with an accurate inventory of the exact uploaded files, row counts, evidence classes, and collection dates. Do not label synthetic fixtures as observed experimental results.

## Intended use
Reproducibility, schema validation, and research into measurement of process-isolation assumptions.

## Limitations
The dataset does not by itself prove that a sandbox is secure or that model weights are confidential. Check each record's `evidence_class`, `status`, and `limitations`.

## Privacy and safety
Remove access tokens, secrets, personal data, private prompts, host-specific sensitive paths, and proprietary model artifacts before publication.
